01 / About

A little about me.

I'm Kevin, an assistant teacher at the University of Idaho Children's Center. My work with young children has deepened my interest in child development and the relationships that make early learning possible.

This portfolio began as part of ECDE 2100: Introduction to Early Childhood Education at the University of Idaho. It brings together coursework, observations, and reflections as I continue developing my teaching practice.

✳My approach centers on emotional regulation, meaningful relationships, curiosity, and giving children room to be children.

03 / Teaching philosophy

Children learn
how to be human.

I believe early childhood education is about more than preparing children for their next classroom. It is about helping them build confidence, navigate emotions, form relationships, and discover who they are.

Free play, experimentation, and relationships matter deeply to me. Structure has its place, but children also need the freedom to explore and make meaning through experience.

An excerpt adapted from my ECDE 2100 teaching philosophy. The full assignment will be added to the appropriate standard once ready for publication.
